# Lesson 2 — Choose and turn an object

**Your goal:** choose between three real 3D objects and look at them from different sides.

Continue with the working starter from [Before you begin](PREPARE_TO_BUILD.md). Keep the same ChatGPT conversation and share your latest code when it changes.

## Make three choices

> Add choices for a tumbler, a robot vacuum and a smart speaker. When I choose one, show its name and a short description.

Save the suggested changes, then build and open the app using the setup guide. Choose each item. Its name and description should change with your choice. Fix any error before adding the next feature.

## Add the objects

Get the three museum models from the [model-copying guide](../curriculum/ADD_MODELS.md). Tell ChatGPT where you put them in your project; typing a filename does not copy the model there.

> Show the matching 3D object when I choose the tumbler, vacuum or speaker. Keep its name and description beside it.

Choose the vacuum: you should see a vacuum and its description. Repeat with the tumbler and speaker. Simple boxes can help you practise, but this step is finished when the three recognizable models appear.

## Look around an object

> Let me turn the object left or right a little at a time. Add Reset to bring it back to the front.

Try the tumbler first: its handle makes turning easy to see. Reset should face it forward without selecting a different object.

Then try the [request for easier viewing](../prompts/PROMPT_LIBRARY.md#make-it-easier-to-see), one option at a time.

## What happened in the original project

At first, the text was too small and one option was cut off. Changing the window’s size made the words readable and exposed the missing control.

[See the original screen after Reset](../evidence/media/l1-reset.png). This earlier picture uses a simpler cup model.

## If you see that problem

Share your screenshot, then describe what is wrong:

> The text is too small and the bottom button is cut off. Make everything readable and keep all the buttons in view.

Check the screen again after the change. If your text already fits, continue without asking for this repair.

## Try it yourself

Turn the speaker and reset it. Is its movement as easy to see as the tumbler’s?
