## Failure, diagnosis and repair

The first fixed-world-scale frame rendered text too small to read. Dynamic world scale improved readability; a taller 1440×1040 dp window exposed the third comfort switch. A light band first mistaken for app chrome was the room shelf visible through glass; the temporary base-panel change was reversed. Opened pixels corrected source assumptions.

Three attempted small-button clicks missed. The agent used the app's actual event landings to derive the run's mapping, then hovered before the counted click. Unchanged admitted frames did not become successful interaction evidence. Those coordinates are specific to the old pose and must not be replayed as calibration.
