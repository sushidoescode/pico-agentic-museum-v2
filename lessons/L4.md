# Lesson 5 — Fill the museum

**Your goal:** grow to eighteen objects, examine them in 3D and visit the archive room.

Continue with your story app. Use [Add the rest of the museum](../curriculum/EXPAND_MUSEUM.md) to download and copy the models, pictures and room. Try each change before continuing.

## Add a few objects

Start with the coffee pod, delivery box and face mask. Share their [collection words](../curriculum/EXPAND_MUSEUM.md#copy-the-collection-words) and tell ChatGPT where their models and pictures are.

> Add these three objects to the museum, using their pictures, models and descriptions. Keep the first three objects working.

Check six choices with matching models. Add the rest in small batches until you have eighteen.

## Find what you want

> Help me find objects by name or collection section. Let me show only the objects I have saved.

Try a name, each collection section and Saved. Search for something absent, then clear the search to bring the collection back.

## Take a closer look

> Let me open the selected object in its own 3D window with turn, reset and close buttons. Close the small information window when I do this.

Try the scooter. Turn it, reset it and close the window; your selection should stay the same. Then add [optional spinning](../prompts/PROMPT_LIBRARY.md#start-and-stop-spinning) and check that Pause stops it.

## Enter the room

Follow [Bring in the room](../curriculum/EXPAND_MUSEUM.md#bring-in-the-room), then share the layout text and the room model’s location.

> Let me enter this room and see one exhibit. Keep a button for returning to the collection easy to find.

Enter, find the exhibit and return. Once that works:

> Put all eighteen objects in the room and let me visit its three areas. I want to get closer to an object and open its 3D view.

Visit each area, examine an object and return to the collection. The original three objects keep their stories; the other fifteen do not have stories.

## What happened in the original project

The room’s first wall labels were too small. They were enlarged, making the sections easier to identify.

Compare [the earlier labels](../evidence/media/l4-before-labels.jpg) with [the later room](../evidence/media/l4-archive.png).

## Try it yourself

Choose an exhibit and guide a friend from the collection to its place in the room and back.
