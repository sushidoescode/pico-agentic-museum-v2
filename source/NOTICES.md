This page explains the main sharing conditions in plain language. It does not grant a new license. The museum as a whole has no blanket open-source license.

## Museum code and art

Project code, writing, original art, and selected screenshots were assembled for the owner's PICO review. The [asset notes](../assets/PROVENANCE_AND_RIGHTS.md) distinguish original Blender models, reference-derived models, and the generated cup. Files available through Drive keep those same conditions.

The three reference-derived models are the fidget toy, scooter, and gas can. The cup was generated with Tripo. Their redistribution and attribution questions remain open. A model's generator label is not enough to establish its rights.

## PICO starter and SDK

The app was made using PICO's volumetric starter. Some Gradle files, Android resources, entry code, and example tests therefore come from that starter. The recorded tools license limits use to developers under agreement with PICO; recipient eligibility and allowed redistribution need confirmation.

SDK binaries and tool caches are not supplied here. The original APK does contain `box.usdz`, a template object, even though visitors do not use it. Older notes saying no template files or reference assets were included were incorrect. The corrected, detailed [notice record](RECORDED_NOTICES.md) preserves the file identities.

The SpatialUI polish module retains its original Apache [LICENSE](../third_party/spatial-ui-polish/LICENSE) and [NOTICE](../third_party/spatial-ui-polish/NOTICE). Its code, tests, and attribution are unchanged. The [module guide](../third_party/POLISH_MODULE.md) records its exact version and the privacy edits made to its documentation.

The Gradle wrapper scripts keep their Apache-2.0 headers. Neither license gives a license to the whole museum or the PICO SDK. No Gradle distribution or dependency cache is included.

## Browser preview, fonts, and audio

The original browser preview included a PICO Web bundle whose separate license scope was not established. That complete preview is excluded. Its project JavaScript, CSS, and HTML shell are retained as design source. The new offline walkthrough is a document showing existing pictures; it is not that preview or a running museum app.

No font binaries are bundled. Earlier generated narration was not accepted or integrated. Screenshots include PICO emulator surroundings and were selected for private technical review, not cleared as public marketing material.
