# Review of the student rewrite

October 7, 2026. This revision responds to feedback that the course was too technical and the prompts sounded unlike ordinary requests.

## What changed

The README, five lessons, prompt library, setup help, glossary, troubleshooting, development timeline, asset/source guides, walkthrough, talk script, and review notes now lead with plain explanations. The lessons use a small goal, a short request, a check, and a follow-up when needed.

There are nineteen new teaching prompts, T01–T19. They are labelled AI-assisted editorial examples, not original human messages or a tested end-to-end recipe. The original P00–P20 library and five earlier lessons remain unchanged in separate files. Sixteen earlier documents were preserved byte for byte as technical or historical references.

A new preparation page separates reading the old source from building with an official starter. A student does not need the unfinished L0 code copied into their project. The free ChatGPT guide explains the manual file-copying workflow and separates subscription access from PICO tool setup.

## What the focused review corrected

One reviewer checked the main learning route, support pages, preserved records, and the separate Luna packet. All five findings were corrected and checked again; none remains unresolved. The corrections were:

- Add the missing bridge from a blank PICO starter to the first museum files.
- Distinguish 34 source exclusions from 37 total package holds.
- Give full relative paths in the code map and link directly to the before/after pictures.
- Correct the Luna report: its panel labelled “inspector” is a text companion, not the original separate 3D inspector.

The new wording has not yet been tested with a classroom or a group of beginners. An editorial review cannot establish how easily real students will complete the course.
