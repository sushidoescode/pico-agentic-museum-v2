# Take a short tour

Open the [picture tour](index.html) and follow these four parts:

1. **Choose an object.** Look at the title, description, and 3D view. They should agree about what you selected.
2. **Compare the second window.** The first version was too small. The larger version made the labels readable; closing and reopening it was a separate check.
3. **Open a story.** The cup, vacuum, and speaker each have three chapters. Return should leave your latest object view alone.
4. **Visit the room.** Look at the bigger wall labels and the way back to the catalogue.

These are saved pictures, not a live app or a continuous video. The [full captions](WALKTHROUGH.md) identify their original versions. For the app download and model files, use the [Drive guide](../ASSET_SUPPLEMENT.md).
