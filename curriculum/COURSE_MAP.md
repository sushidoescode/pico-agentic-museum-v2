## The habit to practise

Ask for a small change. Try it yourself. If something goes wrong, say what you did and what you saw. Check the repair before adding more.

For example:

> Add a button to turn the cup.

After trying it, **if the wrong object moves**:

> I chose the vacuum, but the button still turns the cup. Can you fix that?

These are practice examples. Your next message should describe your own result. Keep [a few notes](MY_BUILD_NOTES.md) so you remember what worked.

The pictures and old source versions show the original project. They do not prove that your new version works. The [saved user messages](../prompts/ACTUAL_USER_MESSAGES.md) and [development story](../process/AI_AND_HUMAN_DECISIONS.md) show what we could recover of its actual progression.
